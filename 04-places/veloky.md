# Veloky

**First Visited**: Session 3

A major walled town in Barovia, governed by Burgomaster [[Vargis]]. The official religion is the Morning Lord — worshipping Pelor is dangerous here.

## Key Locations
- **Vargis's Mansion** — seat of power; where Eva was arrested and pardoned
- **The Inn** — party's resting place
- **Cemetery** — origin point of the [[March of the Dead]]
- **Church** — Eva and Dave rested here (S2)

## Political Situation
- [[Issac]] enforces order, has a personal vendetta against Evangelina
- [[Vargis]] holds power; [[Peppa]] suspects corruption
- [[Finoa]] may have an opposing view on the leadership
- The party was tailed after leaving the mansion (S3)
- [[Victor]] (Vargis's son) reportedly has abilities worth investigating
