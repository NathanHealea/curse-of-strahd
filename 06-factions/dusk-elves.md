# Dusk Elves

**Alignment**: Neutral — sympathetic to the party
**Home**: [[Dusk Elf Settlement]] near the [[Vistani Camp]]

## What the Party Knows
- Present at the [[Vistani Camp]] alongside the Vistani (S4)
- [[Kasimear]] is their leader/representative (S4, S5)
- They remain in Barovia because they **love the land** — it is their home (S5)
- **Straud killed all of their females** — the Dusk Elves are slowly dying out as a people (S5)
- They work with the [[Vistani]] to survive — cooperation out of necessity (S5)
- Kasimear lost his **sister** to Straud (S5)
- A dusk elf gifted a silver ring to "the daughter" — elvish symbols, child-sized, shines brighter than moonlight (S4)

## Stance on Straud
- Kasimear would consider rising against Straud, but needs to see **power strong enough** to stand against him before committing (S5)
- They are not loyal to Straud — they endure him
