## Where We Left Off
Starting in Daggerford with a bounty from Duchess Morwind to hunt werewolves stealing children.

## What Happened
- Party headed east towards the forest following large wolf tracks (about a day old)
- Fog thickened as we went deeper — unnaturally so
- Found a dead man carrying a letter saying the Dursk family had been assaulted by a vampire and needed help
- Came to a path leading to a **dead village**
- Children were whimpering outside a house — said a monster was in the basement and their parents were keeping it there
- They mentioned their baby brother **Walter** was on the 3rd floor
- Entered the Dursk house — golden windmill family symbol, family portrait with the name "Dursk," mother had a scornful look toward the baby
- 2nd floor: smell from the fireplace, tapestry depicting kids fighting off bats
- Found a **hidden room** with books about summoning fiends and a cult of Osiris
- Skeleton in the hidden room killed by poison darts, holding a **letter from Straud** mocking the father as "a worm in his dirt"
- 3rd floor: [[Dagsbrun]] cast Sacred Flame on black armor — it animated and attacked
- Head nurse room with balcony and adjoining nursery — a bundle that seemed like a baby crumbled to dust when checked
- [[bridge]] found a mirror that turned into a stairway going up and down floors
- Master bedroom contained an expensive jewel
- 4th floor: children's room with two **children's skeletons** — they had died of starvation
- A dollhouse in the room was an exact replica of the house
- Windows were bricked up
- Children's ghosts appeared and told us about a **secret passage to the basement** — they still want us to save Walter and kill the monster
