## Where We Left Off

## What Happened
- Eva got brought back up and the party decided to head to **Veloky**
- At midnight, witnessed the **March of the Dead** — 100 ghostly spirits marching from the cemetery to Castle Ravenloft every night. These are the spirits of adventurers who died fighting Straud
- Eva and Dave took a short rest in the church while Bennadick, Peppa, and Dagsbrun went into the village to check on a merchant
- An **old lady** was going door to door handing out pies — [[Bennadick]] stole one from her
- The old lady traded a **young boy** to a family in exchange for 4 "Dream Pastries"
- The 3 crew went to the family's home — they had eaten a pie and seemed to be on drugs
- Party chased after the old lady to save the kid
- [[Dagsbrun]] stopped the cart and tried to hold her, but she shrugged him off
- The old lady revealed herself to be a **Hag**
- The crew of 3 fought the Hag and won — she surrendered the boy
- The Hag agreed to answer questions in exchange for being left alone
- Hag revealed: **Straud has mastery over the land, weather, and spies of the Vistani**

## Combat / Encounters
- **The Hag** — Bennadick, Peppa, and Dagsbrun fought her after she revealed her true form. She surrendered after being beaten
