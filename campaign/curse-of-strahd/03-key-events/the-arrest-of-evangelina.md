# The Arrest of Evangelina

**Session**: 3
**Location**: [[Veloky]]

[[Issac]] recognized and targeted Evangelina upon the party's arrival in Veloky. He took her aside and threatened to kill her if she said Pelor's name again. [[Vargis]] was originally going to burn her, but the party convinced him to let Eva serve as his speaker instead. Eva was tasked with giving a speech to the townspeople. The party received a letter of pardon.

A tense political standoff that revealed the danger of openly worshipping Pelor in Barovia.
