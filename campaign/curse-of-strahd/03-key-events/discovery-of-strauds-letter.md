# Discovery of Straud's Letter

**Session**: 1
**Location**: Hidden room in the Dursk house, [[The Dead Village]]

Found on a skeleton killed by poison darts in a hidden room. The letter was written by [[Straud]] to the Dursk father, mocking him as "a worm in his dirt" and refusing to help his family. Reveals that the Dursk father had previously asked Straud for aid.
