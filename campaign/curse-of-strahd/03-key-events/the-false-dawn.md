# The False Dawn

**Session**: 7
**Location**: Not recorded — probably [[vallaki]], where [[Victor]] lives

Evangelina came to believe she was **the embodiment of Pelor**, his light made flesh in Barovia. If the Dawnfather walked in her, then she could judge souls, and the light would know the innocent from the guilty.

She began to **cleanse** people. Three souls passed through her fire and came out **blessed**. The fourth did not: that person was **turned to ash**.

Then she turned the fire on [[Victor]], the Burgomaster's son, to cleanse his soul too. Victor had [[Vargis]]'s warning about his "abilities" behind him, and he struck first: he **polymorphed Evangelina into a rat**.

When she woke in her own body, [[Bridge]] was the one who named it: Eva had **burned innocents in the name of Pelor**. Afterward, the light within Evangelina had **faded**. She is **not an oathbreaker**, though. Her oath still holds, even if its fire has gone dim.

## Key Outcomes
- **3 blessed, 1 turned to ash** — at least one person died in Eva's cleansing
- **Victor turned Eva into a rat** — his abilities include transmutation magic
- **Bridge confronted Eva**: she burned innocents in Pelor's name
- **The light within Eva has faded** — her divine connection is diminished
- **Eva is not an oathbreaker** — her Oath of Devotion still stands
- "Pelor" is **no longer a safe word** (S7 notes, verbatim)
- The party still plans to meet Victor at the [[blue-water-inn|Blue Water Inn]]

## What It Means for Eva
This is the moment her arc has been heading toward: righteous fire becoming destructive wrath. She did not burn a monster, she burned a person, and she believed the Dawnfather was guiding her hand when she did it. See [[growth|Character Growth]].
