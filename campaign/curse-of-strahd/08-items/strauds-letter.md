# Straud's Letter

**Found**: Session 1
**Location**: Hidden room in the Dursk house, [[The Dead Village]]

A letter written by [[Straud]] to the Dursk father. Found on a skeleton killed by poison darts. Straud mocks the father as "a worm in his dirt" and refuses to help his family despite the father's plea. Reveals that the Dursk family had been assaulted by a vampire and desperately sought aid.
