# Sunblade

**Holder**: [[evangelina|Evangelina Penitentia]]
**First seen in notes**: Session 5

A blade of conjured sunlight carried by [[evangelina|Evangelina]]. On her sheet it deals **1d8+3 radiant** with an extra **1d8 radiant**, and is martial, versatile, finesse, and sap. Lit, it is as much a torch and a symbol as a weapon — the closest thing she owns to a piece of the Dawnfather made manifest, and she treats it that way.

## Known Uses
- **Session 5** — Eva stabbed the fisherman and his boat at [[lake-saraich|Lake Saraich]], sinking the vessel and provoking the tentacled creature in the water ([[rescue-of-arribella|Rescue of Arribella]]).
- Drawing it has its own litany in her [[sayings|sayings]] — *"Drawing the Sunblade"* and the *"Litany of the Sunblade."*

## Open Questions

- Is it the Sunsword of Barovian legend, or an unrelated blade? In S7 the party heard that the **Sun Sword** is a **big name** in Barovia, but nobody has confirmed that Eva's blade is that sword.
- Does the [[order-of-the-silver-dragon|Order of the Silver Dragon]] know anything about it?
