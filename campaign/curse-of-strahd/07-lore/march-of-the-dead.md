# March of the Dead

**First Witnessed**: Session 2
**Location**: Cemetery near [[vallaki]] to [[Castle Ravenloft]]
**Frequency**: Every night at midnight

100 ghostly spirits march from the cemetery to Castle Ravenloft. These are the spirits of adventurers who died fighting [[Straud]]. The party witnessed this on their way to Veloky.

## Significance
Every adventurer who came to Barovia before the party has failed and died. The March is both a warning and a reminder of Straud's power.
