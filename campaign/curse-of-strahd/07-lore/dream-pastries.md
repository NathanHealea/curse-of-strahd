# Dream Pastries

**First Encountered**: Session 2
**Source**: [[The Hag]]

## What the Party Knows
- Sold by the Hag disguised as an old lady going door to door (S2)
- Families trade their **children** for Dream Pastries (S2)
- The pies have a narcotic/hallucinogenic effect — people who eat them appear to be on drugs (S2)
- [[Bennadick]] stole one from the Hag (S2)
- At least one family gave up their young boy for 4 pies (S2)

## Open Questions
- How widespread is Dream Pastry addiction?
- Is the Hag the only supplier, or are there others?
- What exactly do the pastries do — and what happens with prolonged use?
- What does the Hag do with the children she takes?
