# Tarot Card Readings

**Session**: 4
**Location**: [[Vistani Camp]]

The party completed the Diviner and received five card readings:

| Card | Reading | Best Guess |
|------|---------|------------|
| **Necromancer** | *"A woman hangs above a roaring fire. Find her, and you will find the treasure."* | Unknown — who is this woman? |
| **Enchanter** | *"I see a dark figure on a balcony, looking down upon this tortured land with a twisting smile."* | Almost certainly [[Straud]] |
| **Diviner** | *"Look to the one who sees all. The treasure is hidden in her camp."* | The fortune teller? Treasure at the [[Vistani Camp]]? |
| **Donjon** | *"Search for a troubled young man surrounded by wealth and madness. His home is his prison."* | Very likely [[Victor]], son of [[Vargis]] |
| **Darklord** | *"Ah, the worst of all truth: You must face the evil of this land alone."* | Ominous — no outside help is coming |

## Theories & Updates
- The Donjon card strongly matches Victor — surrounded by wealth (Burgomaster's son), madness (yelling for food), and his home may literally be his prison
- **New lead (S5)**: **[[Donjohn]]**, another son of [[Vargis]], may also be connected to the **Donjon** card — the name itself is suspicious. Two possible candidates now: [[Victor]] or Donjohn.
- The Diviner card treasure may be at the Vistani camp itself
- Update this file as cards are resolved or new interpretations emerge
