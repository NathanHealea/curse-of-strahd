# Lake Saraich

**Status**: Visited (S5)
**Location**: North of the [[Vistani Camp]]

## What the Party Knows
- A fisherman used this lake — he was caught trying to throw a sack containing [[Arribella]] into the water (S5)
- [[Evangelina]] shot the fisherman from the dock and sank his boat with the **Sunblade** (S5)
- A **tentacled creature** lives beneath the surface — two tentacles emerged, grabbed Eva and the dead fisherman (S5)
- The fisherman's body was **dragged under** by the creature (S5)
- Eva was thrown and nearly drowned before being rescued (S5)
- The dirty cloth found at the camp smelled of **fish**, linking the kidnapper to this lake (S5)
- There's a **fish shortage**, possibly caused by the creature in the lake (S6)
- Rumors of a **[[the-mad-mage|mad mage]]** roaming north of the lake, in [[mount-baraitoke|Mt. Baraitoke]] (S6)
