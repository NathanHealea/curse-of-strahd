# The Dead Village

**First Visited**: Session 1

An eerie, fog-filled village the party found after following wolf tracks east. Children's ghosts were whimpering outside a house — the Dursk family home. The village appears abandoned except for the spirits.

## The Dursk House
- **Golden windmill** family symbol
- **Family portrait** with the name "Dursk" — mother has a scornful look toward the baby
- **2nd floor**: Smell from the fireplace, tapestry of kids fighting bats
- **Hidden room**: Books on summoning fiends and the [[Cult of Osiris]]. Skeleton killed by poison darts holding [[Straud]]'s letter
- **3rd floor**: Animated armor (triggered by Sacred Flame). Nurse's room with balcony. Nursery — baby bundle crumbled to dust. Mirror that became a stairway up and down
- **Master bedroom**: Expensive jewel found
- **4th floor**: Children's room — two skeletons (died of starvation). Dollhouse that is an exact replica of the house. Windows bricked up. Children's ghosts appeared and revealed a **secret passage to the basement**
- **Basement**: Unexplored. The monster is supposedly here. Children want us to kill it and save [[Walter]]
