# Daggerford

**First Visited**: Session 1

The party's starting location. [[Duchess Morwind]] gave the party the werewolf bounty here — werewolves were stealing children. Other adventurers had already been sent out. The party headed east into the forest, where the fog thickened unnaturally and led them into Barovia.
