# Vistani Camp

**First Visited**: Session 4
**Location**: Outside [[vallaki]]

Home to the [[Vistani]] and dark elves ([[Dusk Elves]]). They remain in Barovia because [[Straud]] allows it, not by choice.

## What Happened Here
- Met [[Kasimear]] — dark elf who shared info about the camp (S4)
- Received tarot card readings (Diviner, Necromancer, Enchanter, Donjon, Darklord)
- Learned about **Lavosh** and **Eribol**
- A woman (Alexia/Elxibela) vanished from the camp — did not wander off
- A daughter has a silver ring: dusk elf gift, elvish symbols, child-sized, shines brighter than moonlight

## Session 5 Events
- Party met with [[Kasimear]] at the nearby [[Dusk Elf Settlement]] — learned Straud killed all the Dusk Elf females (S5)
- Found [[Airbol]] being whipped — his sister [[Arribella]] had been kidnapped (S5)
- [[LaVash]] (father) pledged loyalty to whoever returned his daughter (S5)
- Eva tracked the kidnapper north toward the lake — rescued Arribella from a fisherman (S5)
