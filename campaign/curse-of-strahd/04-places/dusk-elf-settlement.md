# Dusk Elf Settlement

**First Visited**: Session 5
**Location**: Near the [[Vistani Camp]], outside [[vallaki]]

Home to [[Kasimear]] and the remaining [[Dusk Elves]]. Separate from the Vistani camp proper, but the two groups cooperate.

## What the Party Knows
- The Dusk Elves wanted to meet and talk with the party (S5)
- [[Kasimear]] spoke as representative — the Dusk Elves remain because they love the land (S5)
- The settlement houses what remains of the Dusk Elf population — **all females were killed by [[Straud]]**, and they are slowly dying out (S5)
