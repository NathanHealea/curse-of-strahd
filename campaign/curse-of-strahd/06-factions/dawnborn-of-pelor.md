# Dawnborn of Pelor

**Type**: Evangelina's religious order
**Status**: Scattered / destroyed

## What the Party Knows
- Evangelina and her sisters were orphans raised at the Dawnspire Monastery as servants of the Dawnfather Pelor
- Each sister was anointed in sacred flame and blessed with radiant power
- The monastery was destroyed in a surprise attack — talons of shadow, fangs of malice
- The Mother Superior sacrificed herself in a divine blaze that consumed the enemy and the monastery
- Only a handful of sisters survived, including Evangelina and her twin Seraphine
- The surviving Dawnborn scattered across the Sword Coast to hunt for answers
- Evangelina's faith is dangerous in Barovia, where the Morning Lord is the dominant religion
