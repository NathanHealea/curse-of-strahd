## Where We Left Off
Vistani camp outside Veloky — party had just completed the card readings and learned about the missing woman and the Dusk Elves.

## What Happened
- **Donjohn** — the son of [[Vargis]] (Burgomaster of Veloky) — may be connected to the **Donjon** card prophecy
- Arrived at the Dusk Elf settlement near the Vistani camp — the Dusk Elves wanted to meet and talk with us
- Met with **[[Kasimir]]**, a Dusk Elf leader:
  - The Dusk Elves remain in Barovia because they love the land — it is their home
  - **Straud killed all of their females** — the Dusk Elves are slowly dying out as a people
  - They work with the **Vistani** to survive
  - Kasimir lost his **sister** to Straud
  - Eva asked if he would rise against Straud — he said he would need to see power strong enough to stand against him first
- **Private conversation (Bridge only)**: [[Bridge]] asked Kasimir if he had seen her mother — he said no, but promised to keep an eye out. Eva does not know about this.
- Arrived at the Vistani camp proper — found a **young boy being whipped** and talk of a woman who disappeared that morning
- **[[Arribella]]**, a 7-year-old girl, vanished while being watched by her brother **[[Airbol]]** — no sign of where she went or what took her
- **[[LaVash]]** (Arribella's father) said he would do anything to have his daughter returned — asked if he would be more loyal to someone who found her than to Straud, and he said yes
- Eva tracked clues: **barefoot prints** leading to wagons, then **large footprints**, and a **dirty ripped cloth** hanging on a fence pointing north away from camp — it smelled like fish
- Decided to head north toward the lake
- Passed a **guard of Veloky** who said he saw a **fisherman carrying a moving sack** heading back to the lake
- Reached the lake — the fisherman was in a boat, **trying to throw the moving sack into the water**
- **Eva shot the fisherman from the dock**
- The rest of the party reached the fisherman's boat and secured the sack
- Eva stabbed the fisherman and the boat with the **Sunblade**, sinking the vessel
- **A creature in the lake** emerged — **two tentacles** burst from the water, grabbing Eva and the dead fisherman
- Eva was thrown; the fisherman was dragged under the water
- **Eva nearly drowned** — Picalo was thrown to her by Bennadick, and she was saved by rope and pulled into the boat
- The sack contained **Arribella, alive** — Eva got her out and the party returned her to camp

## Combat / Encounters
