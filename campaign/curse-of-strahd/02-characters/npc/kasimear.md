# Kasimear

**Role**: Dusk Elf leader at the [[Dusk Elf Settlement]] near the [[Vistani Camp]]
**Alignment**: Neutral / potential ally
**Race**: [[Dusk Elves|Dusk Elf]]

## What the Party Knows
- Met at the Vistani camp (S4)
- Shared info about the camp and the missing woman (S4)
- The Dusk Elves remain in Barovia because they **love the land** — it is their home (S5)
- **Straud killed all the Dusk Elf females** — the Dusk Elves are slowly dying out as a people (S5)
- The Dusk Elves work with the [[Vistani]] to survive (S5)
- **Lost his sister to Straud** (S5)
- When Eva asked if he would rise against Straud, he said he would need to see **power strong enough** to stand against him before committing (S5)
- [[Bridge]] privately asked if he had seen her mother — he has not, but **promised to keep an eye out** and inform Bridge if he does (S5). Eva does not know about this.
