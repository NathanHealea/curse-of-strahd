# Straud

**Role**: Master of Barovia
**Alignment**: Enemy
**Location**: [[Castle Ravenloft]]

## What the Party Knows
- Described as "the land" itself — has mastery over the land, weather, and spies among the [[Vistani]] (S2, from the Hag)
- Wrote a letter to the Dursk father mocking his "pathetic lineage" and calling him "a worm in his dirt" (S1)
- The [[March of the Dead]] — 100+ spirits of adventurers who died fighting Straud march to Castle Ravenloft every night (S2)
- The **Enchanter card** may refer to him: *"A dark figure on a balcony, looking down upon this tortured land with a twisting smile"* (S4)

## Relationships
- The Hag operates in his domain and shared intel about him when defeated
- The Vistani live in Barovia because he allows it
- [[Issac]] enforces order in [[Veloky]] — unclear if connected to Straud
