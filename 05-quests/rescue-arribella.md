# Rescue Arribella

**Source**: [[LaVash]] at the [[Vistani Camp]] (S5)
**Objective**: Find and rescue [[Arribella]], LaVash's 7-year-old daughter, who vanished from the camp
**Outcome**: **COMPLETE** — Tracked the kidnapper (a fisherman) to the lake north of camp. Eva shot the fisherman, the party secured the sack containing Arribella, and returned her safely to LaVash. A tentacled creature in the lake attacked during the rescue. LaVash pledged his loyalty to the party in return.
