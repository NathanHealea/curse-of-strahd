# Straud's Domain

## What the Party Knows
- [[Straud]] has mastery over the **land** itself (S2, from [[The Hag]])
- He controls the **weather** (S2)
- He has **spies among the [[Vistani]]** (S2)
- The fog that brought the party into Barovia may be under his control (S1)
- The [[March of the Dead]] suggests every adventurer who came before has failed against him (S2)
- He resides in [[Castle Ravenloft]] (S2)
- He mocked the Dursk father as "a worm in his dirt" — treats his subjects with contempt (S1)

## Open Questions
- How far does his control over the land and weather extend?
- Can anyone leave Barovia, or is everyone trapped?
- What is his relationship with the [[Vistani]] — alliance, servitude, or tolerance?
- What are the limits of his power?
