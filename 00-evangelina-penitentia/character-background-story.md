# Character Background Story


Evangelina was not born into an ordinary life. She and her countless sisters were orphans and foundlings, taken in as infants by the Mother Superior of the Dawnspire Monastery and raised as devoted servants of the Dawnfather Pelor. Though no divine blood ran in their veins, each sister was anointed in sacred flame upon her naming day, blessed with a spark of radiant power drawn from Pelor's eternal light. Their home, the Dawnspire Monastery, rose like a beacon of fire and song, its halls resounding with hymns of the Dawnfather's glory.

From childhood, Evangelina lived for duty. Her zeal burned hotter than most of her sisters, her faith uncompromising. Where others offered compassion, she wielded judgment; where others prayed in silence, she preached with fire. Only her twin sister, Seraphine — raised alongside her from the same foundling cradle — softened her edges. Seraphine's warmth was a counterpoint to Evangelina's wrath, mercy beside zeal. Together, they were said to embody both faces of the Dawnfather.

## Then came the night of ruin.

Without warning, darkness swept upon the monastery — talons of shadow, fangs of malice, an army that seemed to rise from nightmare itself. The Dawnborn fought like angels aflame, but the tide was relentless. Evangelina reached the heart of the sanctuary just in time to see her Mother Superior fall, struck down by a monstrous foe cloaked in mist and shadow. With her final breath, their Mother called upon the blessing Pelor had bestowed upon her and unleashed His fury — a divine blaze that consumed the enemy and the monastery alike.

When the fires died, silence remained. Only a handful of sisters survived. Evangelina, Seraphine, and a scattering of kin stood among the ashes, their family, their order, their very home reduced to ruin.

In the wake of tragedy, grief threatened to bind them together — but faith and vengeance pulled them apart. With no answers, only questions, the surviving Dawnborn chose to scatter across the Sword Coast, each vowing to hunt for truth in her own way. Who had dared to strike at Pelor's chosen daughters? What power could defy their Mother and leave only ashes behind?

## Evangelina's path is that of fire.

Guided by her Chaotic Good heart, she wanders where whispers of shadow gather, defending the innocent with blazing conviction while scouring the land for clues to the monastery's fall. Yet her compassion is bound in zeal: to her, all who do not honor Pelor walk in heresy. The ignorant may yet be redeemed by the Dawnfather's warmth, but the stubborn and the wicked must be judged — and burned.

Now she marches alone, her armor scarred but still gleaming with Pelor's sunburst. Every prayer is a vow, every strike a sermon, every battle a chance to carve away the darkness that took her family. Evangelina does not yet know who her enemy is — only that one day, she will find them. And when she does, she will bring the dawn in fire and steel.
