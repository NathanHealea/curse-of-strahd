# Sayings



Healing a comrade.

By the holy fire that burns unyielding, by the Sun that never falters—let Pelor’s light surge within you. Flesh restored, soul purified, strength renewed. Arise, comrade, and carry His glory into battle once more!"

Mercy Killing:

*"Brother… your wounds are beyond my touch, and no healing flame may mend what has been broken. Yet do not despair. To fall in the service of the Radiant One is no defeat, but the highest honor. The shadow cannot claim you, for Pelor’s light already waits to embrace you. Through your blood, the dawn is carried forward. Through your sacrifice, the darkness is driven back.

Go now, into His eternal day, where no night may follow. I, Evangelina, grant you this mercy, not as an end, but as your ascension into His everlasting flame. Rest in the Light, faithful warrior… your watch is done."

My Death:

"*O radiant Pelor, Father of Dawn, your servant’s breath wanes, yet my faith burns undimmed. I go now to the eternal light, unbowed, unbroken. Let my blood be the ink that writes Your glory upon this field. Let my death be the spark that kindles the courage of the faithful. By Your blazing hand, I die not in despair, but in triumph. For in serving You, even death is victory.*"

### **Litany of the Sunblade**

> _Radiant Pelor, Eternal Sun,  
> In Your fire, I stand unbroken.  
> This blade is Your dawn made steel,  
> a flame to scour shadow and sin.  
> Burn fear from my heart,  
> kindle zeal in my hand.  
> By Your light, I strike.  
> By Your light, I endure.  
> By Your light, the night shall end._
