# Fever Dream — Evangelina Burns

Two hallucination scenarios for the session where Evangelina falls to fever. In both, **Eva does not know what is real.** Player-facing lines are what she says out loud; the *DM notes* describe what actually happens.

---

## Scenario 1 — The Dawnfather Walks Among Them

Eva believes she *is* Pelor, come down in flesh to purge Barovia's darkness from its people. She speaks as the god — of herself as "I" and "the Sun," and of Evangelina (if she mentions her at all) as "my daughter." Every touch is a "blessing." Whether that blessing heals or burns is the DM's call.

### Entering the Town

- *"Wake, children of the grey. Your night had ended. The Sun has come down from the sky to walk your streets on foot."*
- *"Do not shield your eyes. Look upon Me. You have lived so long in shadow that you have forgotten My face."*
- *"Open your doors! I do not knock twice. The dawn does not ask permission of the dark."*
- *"I have heard your prayers to the Morning Lord. Sweet children — you were calling My name and did not know how to spell it."*

### The Sermon (Town Square)

> *"Hear Me, Vallaki! For a thousand nights you have bowed to the mist. You have bolted your shutters and fed your children to the fog and called it survival. I call it a wound — and I have come to cauterize it.*
>
> *Within every one of you is a coal of My light, buried under ash. The darkness has crept into your marrow, your breath, your dreams. It wears your faces. It speaks with your tongues. You cannot cut it out alone — but I can burn it out.*
>
> *Some of you will feel My touch as warmth. Some will feel it as fire. Do not be afraid of the fire. Fire is only light that is angry at what hides from it.*
>
> *Come forward. Kneel. Be made clean."*

### The Blessings (Ambiguous by Design)

These lines work whether the result is healing or harm — she says the same words either way.

- *"Kneel, child. Let Me look at you. ...Yes. There it is. The dark is in you, but it is small. Be made clean."*
- *"Hold still. My hand is the morning. It will pass through you like sunlight through a window."*
- *"Receive the Dawn."* (laying a hand on the forehead)
- *"Rise in My name — washed, unburdened, new."*
- *"I knight you in the order of the morning."* (tapping both shoulders with the **Sunblade**, lit)
- *"Open your hands. Take My light and carry it home."* (blessing a group)

### When the Blessing Heals (Lay on Hands / Bless / Shield of Faith)

- *"See! The light knows its own. This one was always Mine."*
- *"Your wounds were the darkness pouring out. Go. Sin no more beneath the moon."*
- *"Feel that? That is what morning was meant to feel like."*
- *"You three — go together and fear nothing. I walk at your shoulder now."* (Bless)

### When the Blessing Burns (Searing / Wrathful / Divine Smite, lit Sunblade)

She hears screams as the darkness fleeing.

- *"Hush, hush. That is not you screaming — that is the shadow being driven out. Let it go."*
- *"The deeper the dark, the hotter the dawn. You held it long, child. It clings."*
- *"Smoke! You see? The night rises out of them like steam from a river."*
- *"Do not pull away from Me. The ones who pull away are the ones it has hold of."*
- *"He fell because the dark was all that held him up. There was nothing else left inside. I am sorry. I am not sorry."*

### When Someone Refuses or Flees

- *"You run from the Sun? Where will you go, little one? Every road in this land leads into night."*
- *"I offered warmth. You chose the cold. Then I shall be the fire instead."*
- *"There is no hiding from morning. It comes for every window."*

### When the Party Intervenes

She does not recognize them as her friends — they look like heralds, priests, or doubting disciples.

- *"Who are you to stay the hand of the Sun? Step aside, or be blessed with them."*
- *"Ah — My little priest. You doubt Me? You have My light in your own palms and still you doubt?"* (to [[Dagsbrun]])
- *"You weep for them? Weep for the darkness, then. It is the only thing dying here."*
- *"Hold My arm one more time, and I will consider it a confession."*
- If someone says *"Eva"* or *"Evangelina"*: *"...My daughter. She sleeps. She has been so tired. Do not wake her. She would not understand what must be done."*

### Cracks in the Vision (Wisdom saves, a calming spell, or a strong roleplay moment)

- *"Why does My hand shake? The Sun does not shake."*
- *"There's... ash on My fingers. The Sun does not leave ash. Does it?"*
- *"Seraphine? ...No. No, you are not her. She is not here. Why is no one here?"*
- *"I am so hot. I am the Sun and I am burning."*

### When the Fever Breaks

- *"...Where is my sword? Why is it out? Why are they looking at me like that?"*
- *"Dawnfather... what did I do in Your name?"*
- *(quietly, to the party)* *"Tell me plainly. How many did I heal — and how many did I burn?"*

> **DM notes — mechanics**
> - Evangelina is a **Paladin 6 (Oath of Devotion)**: Lay on Hands pool = **30 HP**, four 1st-level and two 2nd-level slots, spell save **DC 14**, **Aura of Protection** (+3 to saves within 10 ft.), [[sunblade|Sunblade]] in hand. *Burning Hands* is not on her spell list — if you want that effect, treat a lit Sunblade "knighting" as a melee hit, or have her trigger something environmental (a torch, a brazier, a thatched roof).
> - Optional random table per blessing (d8): **1–2** Lay on Hands, **3** Bless, **4** Shield of Faith, **5** Searing Smite / lit Sunblade, **6** Divine Smite, **7** Aid, **8** Zone of Truth — she demands the truth and is terrified of the answer.
> - Lean the roll toward *burn* when the target is afraid, resists, or worships the Morning Lord loudly; toward *heal* when the target is a child or genuinely kneels. Eva's subconscious is choosing even if she isn't.
> - Echo: this is exactly what her Mother Superior did — divine fire that took friend and foe alike — and what she did to the coffin maker. Let a villager or party member say so afterward.

## Scenario 2 — The Night of Ruin, Again

Leaving town, the fever drags her back into the night the Dawnspire Monastery fell. **The party look like her sisters.** Everyone else — villagers, travelers, a Vistani wagon, livestock, even a scarecrow — is one of the attackers. She knows *what* she fought that night, not *who* sent them.

### Party as Sisters (suggested casting)

| Party member | Who Eva sees |
|---|---|
| [[Dagsbrun]] | **Seraphine** — her twin, the mercy beside her zeal |
| [[Peppa]] | **Sister Lucienne** — the youngest novice, always lagging behind |
| [[Bennadick]] | **Sister Maribel** — the bell-ringer, big and loud and brave |
| [[bridge]] | **Sister Ondine** — the quiet archivist who kept the relic-keys |

### The Bells

- *"The bells — do you hear the bells? Why are they ringing at this hour? SERAPHINE, get up!"*
- *"Lucienne, behind me. Stay behind me. Do not look at the courtyard."*
- *"Maribel, ring it again! Ring it until your arms break — wake every sister in this house!"*
- *"Ondine — the sanctuary. Mother is in the sanctuary. We have to reach her."*

### Calling the Charge

- *"Dawnborn! To me! Form the line — shoulder to shoulder, flame to flame!"*
- *"They came in the dark because they knew they could not face us in the day. Let us show them a dawn at midnight!"*
- *"Sisters, sing! Sing the Hymn of First Light — they cannot bear it, I have seen them flinch!"*
- *"No one falls tonight. Do you hear me? No one else falls tonight."*

### Against the Things She Fought

**The Fangs** (wolves, dogs, horses, anything on four legs)
- *"Wolves in the cloister! Burn them back — they fear the fire!"*
- *"This one stood up. Seraphine, it STOOD UP like a man. Kill it before it speaks."*
- *"Down, beast. You will not take another sister's throat."*

**The Talons** (birds, anything above, people on rooftops or ladders)
- *"Above us! The winged ones are on the bell tower — Maribel, get DOWN!"*
- *"They drop from the dark like stones. Shields up, sisters!"*

**The Grey Acolytes** (anyone robed, cloaked, hooded, or chanting — including priests of the Morning Lord)
- *"Grey robes. Grey robes and that chanting. Stop your mouth, heretic, or I will stop it with steel!"*
- *"That mark on your hand — I have seen it. I killed one wearing it in the scriptorium. I will kill you too."*
- *"Your prayers made the dark come in. Pray now, and see who answers."*

**The Hollow Ones** (anyone who moves slowly, stumbles, or is drunk or wounded)
- *"They do not bleed. Why don't they bleed? Strike the head — the head!"*
- *"That was Sister Agathe. That WAS Sister Agathe. I'm sorry. I'm so sorry. Go back to the light."*

**The Mist-Cloaked One** (anyone tall in a dark cloak, anyone standing in fog — especially if fog rolls in)
- *"YOU. You stood over her. You stood over our Mother while she bled."*
- *"Come out of the mist, coward. Show me a face so I can burn it."*
- *"She burned you. I SAW her burn you. Why are you still standing?"*
- *"Seraphine, run. Take the little ones and run. This one is mine."*

### Seraphine (Dagsbrun) Tries to Stop Her

- *"Not now, sister. Mercy is for after. There is no after if we lose tonight."*
- *"You always want to save them. Look around you! Look what they did to us!"*
- *"Let go of my arm, Seraphine. Please. Please, I can't lose you too."*
- If Dagsbrun says something only Seraphine would say: *"...You're crying. You never cry. ...Why are your hands wrong? Your hands are not her hands."*

### Mother Superior Falls (the climax of the hallucination)

- *"MOTHER! No — no, no, get up. Get up, you are the Dawn, you cannot fall —"*
- *"Don't. Don't you dare call the fire. We can still win, Mother, PLEASE —"*
- *(the light)* *"...It's so bright. It's all so bright. Everything is burning and it's beautiful and I can't —"*

### Waking in the Ashes

- *"...Where is the monastery? Where did the walls go?"*
- *"There were so many of them. I killed so many of them. ...Why do they have Barovian faces?"*
- *"Seraphine? ...Dagsbrun. You're Dagsbrun. Then what — who —"*
- *"Dawnfather, I fought that night twice now. Do not make me lose it twice."*

## DM Only — Who Burned the Dawnspire

*Eva does not know any of this. She knows only the things she fought and killed. Use, change, or discard freely — nothing here is from the module.*

### The Mastermind: Ysolde Varn, the Lightless Abbess

- **Once the Dawnspire's first foundling** and the Mother Superior's chosen heir. Brilliant, beloved, and the most zealous sister the monastery ever raised — the Evangelina of a generation before.
- **Her fall:** She led sisters to "purify" a village of heretics and burned it with everyone inside. The Mother Superior stripped her of her flame-anointing and cast her out. Ysolde believed Pelor's light had been taken from her by a weak and merciful woman.
- **In exile** she found the **[[cult-of-osiris|Cult of Osiris]]** — death-and-rebirth mystics who promised that a fire which could not be taken away could be found on the far side of death. She died, and came back cold.
- **Her goal:** The **Ember of First Dawn**, a relic of Pelor's light kept in the sanctuary's heart, which she believed was her birthright. With it she meant to make a "dawn that never ends" — a sun that burns without mercy, forever.
- **Why it matters for Eva:** Ysolde is what Evangelina becomes if the righteous-fire/wrathful-fire question is answered the wrong way. Every time Eva burns someone who could have been saved, she walks one step closer to Ysolde's path. The cult materials found in the Dursk house (S1) can be a quiet first breadcrumb.

### Her Forces (What Eva Fought)

| What Eva remembers | What it actually was |
|---|---|
| **Fangs** — wolves that stood up like men | A **werewolf pack** paid in Cult blood-rites. Remnants fled into the fog... and the party came to Barovia hunting werewolves from Daggerford. |
| **Talons** — winged things on the bell tower | Shadow-bound **gargoyle-like constructs** made from the monastery's own stolen roof statuary. |
| **Grey Acolytes** — chanting robed figures with a hand-mark | **Cult of Osiris initiates**, branded on the palm with an eye-and-ankh sigil. |
| **Hollow Ones** — sisters who did not bleed | **Fallen Dawnborn raised mid-battle** by the acolytes. Eva cut down at least one she knew (Sister Agathe). |
| **The Mist-Cloaked One** — who killed Mother Superior | Ysolde's lieutenant, **the Grey Shepherd** — a vampiric undead who dissolved into mist as the blaze struck. **It survived.** |

### Hooks

- **A survivor in Barovia:** One of the werewolves Eva wounded that night is in Barovia and remembers her face.
- **The sigil:** The eye-and-ankh mark could turn up on an NPC's palm, in the Dursk house books, or at the [[the-winery|Winery]].
- **The Ember:** The Mother Superior's blaze may not have destroyed it. Ysolde may still be looking — or may already have it.
- **Red herring:** The Mist-Cloaked One's mist-form will make Eva (and the players) suspect [[Straud]]. Keep that ambiguous as long as it's fun.
- **During the fever:** Have Eva shout one *true* detail she has never consciously remembered — *"The mark on your palm — the eye with a cross beneath it!"* — so the party learns something real from the hallucination.
